Rotate your device

Play El Pollo Loco in landscape mode.

Controls

  • Left Arrow – Pepe moves left
  • Right Arrow – Pepe moves right
  • Up Arrow – Pepe jumps
  • Space – open level selection in the menu; hold and release to throw in-game
  • P / Pause – pause / resume the game
  • Jump onto chickens from above – defeat enemies
  • Fullscreen – expand the game
